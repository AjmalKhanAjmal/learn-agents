embedder.py
======================

For very high-throughput systems, I would extend this with:

Lazy model initialization to reduce startup time.
Batch-size configuration to optimize GPU/CPU utilization.
Health checks to verify the model is loaded correctly.
Caching for repeated chunks.
An asynchronous embedding queue for long-running ingestion jobs.
Metrics (latency, throughput, failures) exported to Prometheus or OpenTelemetry.
