Key takeaways about the Context Window:

Evolution of capacity: Earlier models like GPT-1 (2022) had a context 
window of only 512 tokens, while modern models like GPT-6 (asra) or
 Claude can handle up to 1 million tokens.
What gets stored: The window tracks the user's input, 
the LLM's generated response, all previous messages in the current 
conversation, and any uploaded documents or images
.
Performance degradation: It is generally recommended not to fill the 
context window to its maximum capacity (e.g., stay below 80-85%)
