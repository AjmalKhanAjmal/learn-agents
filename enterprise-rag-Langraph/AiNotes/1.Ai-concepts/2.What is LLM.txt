-->LLMs, predict the next token. And how do they predict the next token?
 By using a lot of math. There is a lot of math involved in predicting
  which next word has the highest probability of appearing after a word.
 It requires a lot of data to recognize patterns in human language, 
 which allows LLMs to identify that 
  if 'Capital of India' is written, there is a high probability delhi  
  'is' should come after it. There is a very low probability that 
  'apple' or 'mango' would directly print after 'Delhi'. So, in this 
  way, it is all a game of probabilities—which is the next best word 
  that will fit into our proper response.

And LLMs work on this exact logic to generate a response for us."
